Source dataset: https://male-cns.janelia.org/download/

Derived from Fruitless assets/circuit.json and assets/context.json. We retain the 124 selected body IDs and 1,106 direct anatomical edges and their synapse counts. Neurite samples are decimated threefold; context soma coordinates are filtered to the brain region. Geometry remains in 8 nm voxel coordinates. Context points are not simulated neurons.

Our live leaky integrate-and-fire rules, sensory encoding, output gain, and tank controls are authored game adaptations, not measured physiology or a reproduction of Fruitless's courtship experiment. The inhibitory sign is a modeling assumption; anatomical counts are measured. No upstream endorsement is implied.
